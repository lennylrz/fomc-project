The Committee chose to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

Regarding the economic situation, the Committee assesses that the current monetary policy approach, alongside strong underlying productivity gains, continues to significantly bolster economic activity. Recent data since the last meeting confirms a rapid expansion in output, and the employment situation shows modest improvement. Core consumer price increases are subdued and are anticipated to remain low.

Looking ahead, the Committee views the potential for stronger or weaker sustainable growth over the coming quarters as roughly balanced. The likelihood of an undesirable decline in inflation has decreased recently and is now seen as nearly equivalent to the chance of an inflation increase. Nevertheless, given the low inflation environment and underutilized economic resources, the Committee believes that the current supportive policy stance can be sustained for an extended duration.
