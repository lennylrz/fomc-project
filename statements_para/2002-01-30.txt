Today, the Committee opted to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

Evidence suggests a reduction in economic sluggishness, with activity showing initial signs of strengthening. As factors hindering the economy recede, and given the positive long-term outlook for productivity gains alongside supportive monetary conditions, the prospects for an economic rebound appear brighter.

Nevertheless, the extent of future vigor in business investment and household consumption remains unclear. Consequently, the Committee continues to assess that, considering its objectives for stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on present data, the predominant risks lean towards potential economic softness in the near term.
