The Committee has opted to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent data indicates that economic activity has received a significant boost from a substantial shift in inventory investment. However, the extent to which final demand will strengthen in the coming quarters, which is crucial for a sustained economic expansion, remains unclear.

Given these conditions, and despite the current supportive monetary policy stance, the Committee judges that, looking ahead, the risks to achieving both its long-term objectives of stable prices and sustainable economic growth are evenly distributed, based on the information presently available.

No members dissented from this decision.
