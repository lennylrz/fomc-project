The Committee has opted to decrease its primary policy interest rate by half a percentage point. Concurrently, the Board of Governors sanctioned a reduction in the discount rate by an equivalent amount. The central bank will persist in providing substantial liquidity to financial markets as necessary, until market operations normalize. Consequently, the Committee acknowledges that the actual federal funds rate might occasionally fall below its target under these exceptional circumstances.

Prior to recent tragic events, the labor market, industrial output, and corporate investment were already subdued, and these events could further depress spending. Despite this, the long-term outlook for economic efficiency and overall economic health remains positive and should become apparent once current constraints on demand diminish. Looking ahead, the Committee maintains its view that, considering its long-term objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current data, the predominant risks lean towards economic underperformance.

The Federal Reserve Board's decision on the discount rate followed approvals from the Boards of Directors of the Federal Reserve Banks of Richmond, Chicago, Minneapolis, Dallas, and San Francisco.
