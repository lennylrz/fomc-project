Today, the Committee increased its primary policy rate by a quarter of a percentage point.

This adjustment leaves monetary policy still supportive, and, alongside strong productivity gains, continues to bolster economic activity. Economic output appears to be expanding at a moderate rate, even with the prior increase in energy costs. The employment situation continues its gradual improvement. Both current inflation and long-term inflation expectations remain under control.

The Committee views the potential for both stronger and weaker outcomes for sustained growth and stable prices over the coming quarters as roughly balanced. Given that underlying inflation is anticipated to be relatively low, the Committee believes that the removal of policy support can proceed at a measured speed. However, the Committee will adjust its approach as economic conditions evolve to meet its commitment to price stability.

Separately, the Board of Governors unanimously endorsed a quarter percentage point rise in the discount rate. This decision followed requests from the Boards of Directors of numerous Federal Reserve Banks.

Furthermore, the Committee unanimously agreed to accelerate the publication of its meeting records. Starting with this meeting, the minutes from scheduled meetings will be released three weeks after the policy decision date.
