The Committee has opted to retain its current monetary policy settings, leaving the federal funds rate target unaltered.

Recent economic figures suggest that the overall demand for goods and services is slowing, aligning more closely with the economy's capacity for production. These same figures also point to faster gains in output per worker, which is boosting that productive capacity and simultaneously helping to control expenses and temper fundamental price increases.

Despite these developments, the Committee remains watchful regarding the potential for demand to outstrip available supply, especially given that the employment of the workforce is currently at an exceptionally high level.

Considering its objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on the information at hand, the Committee judges that the likelihood of increased inflationary pressures in the near term continues to be the predominant concern.
