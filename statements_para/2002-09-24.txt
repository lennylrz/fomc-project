The Committee has opted to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent economic data indicates that overall demand within the economy is expanding at a moderate rate.

Looking ahead, the present supportive monetary conditions, alongside a sustained strong underlying increase in productivity, are anticipated to facilitate an improved economic environment. Nevertheless, significant ambiguity remains regarding the magnitude and timing of the projected acceleration in output and job creation, partly due to the emergence of elevated geopolitical concerns.

Therefore, the Committee judges that, for the foreseeable future, considering its long-term objectives of stable prices and enduring economic expansion, and based on currently available information, the predominant risks lean towards conditions that could lead to economic deceleration.
