Nevertheless, current indicators suggest ongoing weakness in the labor market, with unemployment remaining elevated. Household spending has recently picked up pace. Inflation appears to have moderated since earlier in the year, as energy and some commodity prices have fallen from their peaks. Long-term inflation expectations have remained stable.

There are significant downside risks to the economic outlook, including stress in global financial markets. The Committee also anticipates that inflation will settle at or below levels consistent with its dual mandate in the coming quarters, as the effects of past energy and commodity price increases further diminish. However, the Committee will closely monitor the evolution of inflation and inflation expectations.
