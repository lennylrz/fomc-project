The Committee decided to keep its policy interest rate unchanged.

Recent data indicate that economic activity is no longer declining. Financial market conditions have improved further. Consumer spending shows signs of stabilizing, though it is still held back by job losses, slow income growth, reduced housing wealth, and restricted credit. Businesses are still reducing investment and employment, but they are making progress in aligning inventories with sales.

While economic activity is expected to remain weak for a period, the Committee foresees a gradual return to sustainable growth with stable prices, supported by financial stabilization efforts, fiscal and monetary stimulus, and market dynamics. Recent increases in energy and commodity prices are noted. However, significant underutilization of resources should limit cost pressures, and inflation is expected to remain low for some time.

The Federal Reserve will utilize all available instruments to foster economic recovery and maintain price stability. The Committee expects that economic circumstances will necessitate exceptionally low policy rates for an extended duration. To support mortgage lending, housing markets, and overall private credit, the Federal Reserve will complete its previously announced purchases of agency mortgage-backed securities and agency debt by year-end. The ongoing purchases of Treasury securities will be gradually slowed, with the full amount acquired by the end of [month], to ensure a smooth market transition. The Committee will continuously assess the timing and total volume of its security purchases based on the evolving economic outlook and financial market conditions. The Federal Reserve is also monitoring its balance sheet and will adjust its credit and liquidity programs as needed.
