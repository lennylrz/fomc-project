The Committee has opted to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent data indicates a moderate expansion of the economy, despite a deceleration in global economic activity. While labor market indicators show some improvement, the unemployment rate remains high. Consumer spending has progressed, but business investment has slowed, and the housing sector remains weak. Inflation has been low recently, and long-term inflation expectations are stable.

The Committee foresees modest economic growth in the coming quarters, leading to a gradual reduction in the unemployment rate towards levels consistent with its objectives. Significant downside risks to the economic outlook persist due to global financial market pressures. The Committee also expects inflation to remain at or below its mandated levels in the coming quarters.

To foster a more robust economic recovery and ensure inflation aligns with its objectives over time, the Committee intends to keep monetary policy highly accommodative. The Committee will continue its program to lengthen the average maturity of its securities portfolio. It will also maintain its current practices of reinvesting principal payments from agency debt and mortgage-backed securities into agency mortgage-backed securities, and rolling over maturing Treasury securities. The Committee will periodically assess its securities holdings and adjust them as needed to support a stronger recovery and price stability.
