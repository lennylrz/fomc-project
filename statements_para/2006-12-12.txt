The Committee chose to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Economic expansion has decelerated throughout the current year, partly due to a significant slowdown in the housing sector. Despite varied recent data, the economy is projected to grow at a moderate rate in the upcoming quarters.

Underlying inflation measures have been high, and the economy's full capacity utilization could sustain inflationary pressures. Nevertheless, inflation is expected to ease over time, driven by lower energy price impacts, stable inflation expectations, and the combined effects of monetary policy and other factors limiting overall demand.

However, the Committee believes that some inflation-related risks persist. The necessity, magnitude, and timing of any further policy tightening to address these risks will be determined by how the economic and inflation outlooks evolve, based on new information.
