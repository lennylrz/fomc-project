The Federal Open Market Committee has decided to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

This action is being taken to ensure the smooth operation of financial markets by supplying necessary liquidity. The central bank will continue to inject reserves into the system through its open market operations as needed. This is intended to keep the federal funds market trading at rates consistent with the Committee's target. Given the current disruptions in money and credit markets, financial institutions may encounter unexpected funding requirements. The discount window remains accessible as a source of such funding.
