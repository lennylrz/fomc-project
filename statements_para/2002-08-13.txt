The Committee has opted to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Economic activity has experienced a sustained deceleration, largely attributable to instability in financial markets and increased apprehension stemming from issues in corporate transparency and oversight.

Despite these challenges, the existing supportive monetary policy, alongside robust underlying productivity gains, is anticipated to facilitate an eventual improvement in the economic environment.

However, looking ahead, and considering the Committee's objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, the prevailing information suggests that the predominant risks lean towards a potential for economic underperformance.
