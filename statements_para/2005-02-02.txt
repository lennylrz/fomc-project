The Committee has opted to increase its primary policy rate by a quarter of a percentage point.

This adjustment notwithstanding, the current monetary policy stance is still seen as supportive, contributing to economic activity alongside strong productivity gains. Economic output is expanding at a moderate rate, even with elevated energy costs, and the employment situation is steadily getting better. Both current inflation and expectations for future inflation over the longer term are well-managed.

Looking ahead, the Committee judges that the potential for both stronger and weaker outcomes for sustained growth and stable prices over the coming quarters are roughly balanced. Given that underlying inflation is anticipated to stay relatively low, the Committee believes that policy adjustments can proceed at a gradual pace. However, the Committee stands ready to alter its approach if economic conditions shift, in order to meet its commitment to price stability.
