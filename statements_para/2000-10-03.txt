The Committee has opted to maintain its current monetary policy settings, leaving the federal funds rate target unchanged.

Economic activity has recently shown signs of decelerating, aligning more closely with the economy's capacity for growth. Furthermore, ongoing improvements in productivity are helping to mitigate cost pressures and restrain underlying price increases.

Nevertheless, the labor market continues to operate at a very high level of employment. While recent energy price increases have not significantly impacted core inflation measures thus far, they present a potential risk for future inflation expectations. The stability of these expectations has been crucial in fostering an environment supportive of sustained economic expansion.

Considering its objectives of stable prices and long-term economic growth, and based on current information, the Committee assesses that the primary risks remain tilted towards future inflationary pressures.
