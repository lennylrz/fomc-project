The Committee decided to keep its policy interest rate unchanged. It will also continue its program of asset purchases as previously announced in [month], maintaining its current practice of reinvesting principal payments from its securities. The plan involves acquiring $600 billion in longer-term Treasury securities by the close of the second quarter of [this year]. The Committee will routinely assess the speed of these purchases and the total volume of the program based on new information, making adjustments as needed to best support its objectives of maximum employment and price stability.

Recent data indicates an improving economic recovery, with gradual betterment in the labor market. Household spending and business investment in equipment and software are growing. However, investment in nonresidential structures remains weak, and the housing sector is still struggling. Commodity prices have increased significantly since the summer, and concerns about global crude oil supplies have led to a sharp rise in oil prices recently. Despite this, long-term inflation expectations have remained stable, and underlying inflation measures are subdued.

The unemployment rate remains high, and underlying inflation measures are somewhat low compared to levels consistent with the Committee's long-term objectives. While recent increases in energy and other commodity prices are currently pushing inflation higher, these effects are expected to be temporary. The Committee will closely monitor inflation and inflation expectations. A gradual return to higher resource utilization is anticipated, consistent with price stability.

The Committee expects that economic conditions, including low resource utilization, subdued inflation, and stable inflation expectations, will necessitate exceptionally low policy rates for an extended period. The Committee will continue to observe the economic outlook and financial developments, using its tools as needed to support the recovery and ensure inflation aligns with its mandate over time.
