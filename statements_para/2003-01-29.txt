The Committee has opted to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Businesses are reportedly holding back on investment and hiring due to elevated oil prices and other geopolitical uncertainties. However, the Committee anticipates that once these risks subside, as generally expected, the current supportive monetary policy, alongside sustained productivity gains, will foster a gradual improvement in economic conditions.

Considering these factors, the Committee judges that, in light of its objectives for stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current information, the risks to achieving both these goals are evenly weighted for the foreseeable future.
