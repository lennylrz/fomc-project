The Committee has opted to increase the federal funds rate target by a quarter of a percentage point.

This adjustment notwithstanding, the current monetary policy stance continues to be supportive, fostering economic activity alongside strong productivity gains. Economic output is expanding at a moderate rate, even with elevated energy costs, and the employment situation has improved. Both current inflation and long-term inflation expectations are well-managed.

Looking ahead a few quarters, the Committee assesses that the potential for stronger or weaker economic growth and price stability are approximately balanced. Given that underlying inflation is anticipated to remain relatively low, the Committee believes that the process of reducing monetary accommodation can proceed at a gradual pace. However, the Committee stands ready to adjust policy in response to evolving economic conditions to meet its commitment to price stability.

Separately, the Board of Governors unanimously endorsed a quarter percentage point increase in the discount rate. This decision followed requests from the Boards of Directors of several Federal Reserve Banks.
