The Committee has opted to increase its primary policy rate by a quarter of a percentage point.

This adjustment notwithstanding, the Committee assesses that the current monetary policy stance continues to be supportive, and when combined with strong productivity gains, it is fostering economic expansion. Despite further increases in energy costs, the economy's growth trajectory remains solid, and the employment situation is steadily improving. While inflationary pressures persist at an elevated level, long-term inflation expectations are stable.

The Committee anticipates that, through suitable policy interventions, the potential for both sustained growth and price stability can be balanced. Given the expectation of contained underlying inflation, the Committee believes that the removal of accommodative policy can proceed at a gradual pace. However, the Committee stands ready to adjust its approach in response to evolving economic conditions to uphold its commitment to price stability.

Separately, the Board of Governors unanimously endorsed a 25-basis-point increase in the discount rate. This action followed the approval of requests from the Boards of Directors of numerous Federal Reserve Banks.
