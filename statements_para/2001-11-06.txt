The Federal Open Market Committee has opted to decrease its benchmark interest rate by half a percentage point. Concurrently, the Board of Governors sanctioned a corresponding reduction of 0.5 percentage points in the discount rate.

Economic activity is currently being suppressed by elevated levels of uncertainty and apprehension regarding a worsening of business conditions, both domestically and internationally. Consequently, the Committee maintains its view that, considering its objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current information, the predominant risks lean towards scenarios that could lead to economic fragility for the foreseeable future.

While the essential redirection of resources for security purposes might temporarily impede productivity gains, the long-term outlook for productivity growth and the broader economy remains positive and is expected to materialize once the extraordinary factors constraining demand subside.

The Federal Reserve Board's approval of the discount rate adjustment followed a request from the Board of Directors of the Federal Reserve Bank of Richmond.
