The Committee chose to keep its policy interest rate unchanged at this meeting. This decision reflects current and anticipated conditions in the job market and price stability. The current monetary policy approach continues to support robust employment and the sustained achievement of the 2 percent inflation target.

Recent information indicates continued strengthening in the labor market and a moderate expansion of economic activity. Job creation has been robust on average over recent months, and the unemployment rate has remained low. Household spending growth appears to have slowed from its strong pace in the fourth quarter, while business fixed investment continued its strong upward trend. Over the past year, both overall inflation and core inflation have approached 2 percent. Market-based indicators of inflation compensation are still low, and survey-based measures of long-term inflation expectations show little overall change.

The Committee anticipates that, with additional measured adjustments to monetary policy, economic growth will proceed at a moderate rate over the medium term, and labor market strength will persist. Inflation, measured over a 12-month period, is projected to be near the Committee's symmetric 2 percent objective in the medium term. The risks to the economic outlook appear to be generally balanced.

Future adjustments to the policy rate will be determined by the Committee's evaluation of actual and projected economic conditions against its goals of maximum employment and 2 percent inflation. This evaluation will consider various data, including labor market indicators, inflation pressures and expectations, and financial and international developments. The Committee will closely watch actual and expected inflation relative to its symmetric inflation target. The Committee expects that economic conditions will necessitate further gradual increases in the policy rate, which is likely to remain below its long-run equilibrium for some time. However, the actual trajectory of the policy rate will depend on the economic outlook as new data become available.
