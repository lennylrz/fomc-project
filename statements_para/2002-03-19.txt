The Committee maintained its policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent data suggest a notable acceleration in economic activity, largely driven by a substantial shift in inventory accumulation. However, the extent to which underlying demand will strengthen in the coming quarters, which is crucial for sustained economic growth, remains unclear.

Given these conditions, and considering the current supportive monetary policy stance, the Committee judges that the risks to achieving its long-term objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion are evenly balanced for the foreseeable future, based on available information.

The Committee has opted to immediately disclose the voting record for the policy interest rate decision in its post-meeting announcements, including the preferred policy of any dissenting members. This change provides this information sooner than its previous release in the meeting minutes. In line with this new procedure, the Board of Governors will also promptly publish the voting record for any discount rate decisions, noting any dissenting preferences.
