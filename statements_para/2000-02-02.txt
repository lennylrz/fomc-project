The Committee decided to increase its primary policy interest rate by a quarter of a percentage point. Concurrently, the Board of Governors endorsed a similar upward adjustment of a quarter of a percentage point to the discount rate.

The Committee continues to hold the view that, over an extended period, the expansion of aggregate demand is likely to outpace the economy's capacity to produce goods and services, even when considering the significant acceleration in productivity growth. This dynamic could lead to inflationary pressures that would jeopardize the current period of sustained economic expansion.

Considering its objectives for stable prices and enduring economic growth, and based on current data, the Committee assesses that the likelihood of future conditions leading to increased inflation is elevated.
