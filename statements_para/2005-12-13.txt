The Committee chose to increase its primary policy interest rate by a quarter of a percentage point.

Economic activity shows robust growth, even with the impact of higher energy costs and recent hurricane events. Underlying inflation has remained subdued over the past few months, and expectations for long-term price increases are stable. However, potential increases in how much the economy is producing, alongside elevated energy prices, could contribute to future inflationary pressures.

The Committee believes that additional, gradual adjustments to policy will probably be necessary to maintain a good balance between achieving sustained economic expansion and stable prices. Regardless, the Committee stands ready to adjust its approach based on evolving economic conditions to meet these goals.

Separately, the Board of Governors unanimously endorsed a quarter percentage point rise in the discount rate. This decision followed requests from the boards of directors of twelve Federal Reserve Banks.
