The Committee chose to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

This decision reflects the Committee's view that the present monetary policy approach, alongside strong underlying productivity gains, continues to significantly bolster economic activity. Recent data since the last meeting indicates a strengthening in consumer and business outlays, and the employment situation appears to be settling. Firms' ability to raise prices and core consumer price increases remain subdued.

Looking ahead, the Committee assesses that the potential for stronger-than-expected economic expansion and the potential for weaker-than-expected expansion are roughly balanced over the coming quarters. However, the slight chance of an undesirable decline in inflation is greater than the chance of an increase from its already low level. Overall, the Committee concludes that the risk of inflation falling too low remains the primary concern for the foreseeable future. Given these conditions, the Committee anticipates that the current supportive policy stance can be sustained for an extended duration.
