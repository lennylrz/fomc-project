The Committee chose to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

Economic activity showed expansion during the second quarter, driven in part by increased household consumption and international trade. Nevertheless, the employment situation has continued to weaken, and financial markets are still experiencing significant strain. Restrictive lending conditions, the persistent downturn in the housing sector, and elevated energy costs are anticipated to restrain economic expansion over the coming quarters. Over time, the considerable loosening of monetary policy, alongside ongoing efforts to enhance market liquidity, should contribute to fostering moderate economic growth.

Inflation has been elevated, fueled by previous surges in energy and certain other commodity prices, and some measures of inflation expectations have risen. The Committee foresees inflation easing later this year and into [year+1], but the future path of inflation remains highly unpredictable.

While risks to economic contraction persist, the Committee is also significantly concerned about the potential for higher inflation. The Committee will continue to observe economic and financial trends and will take necessary actions to support sustained economic growth and price stability.
