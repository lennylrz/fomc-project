The Committee decided to maintain the existing target range for the federal funds rate. Additionally, the program to extend the average maturity of the institution's securities holdings will persist until the close of the year, and the current practice of reinvesting principal payments from agency debt and mortgage-backed securities into agency mortgage-backed securities will continue.

Recent information indicates that economic expansion slowed somewhat during the first half of the year. Job creation has been sluggish in recent months, and the jobless rate remains elevated. Business fixed investment has continued to advance. Household spending has been increasing at a somewhat slower pace than earlier in the year. The housing market, despite some further signs of improvement, remains weak. Price increases have moderated since earlier this year, primarily due to lower costs for crude oil and gasoline, and long-term inflation expectations have held steady.

The Committee anticipates that economic growth will remain moderate in the coming quarters, followed by a very gradual acceleration. Consequently, the Committee expects the unemployment rate to decline only slowly toward levels consistent with its mandate. Furthermore, stress in global financial markets continues to present considerable downside risks to the economic outlook. The Committee foresees inflation over the medium term running at or below its target rate.

To support a more robust economic recovery and to help ensure inflation is at its target rate over time, the Committee intends to maintain a highly accommodative monetary policy stance. The Committee currently expects that economic conditions, including underutilized resources and a subdued medium-term inflation outlook, will likely justify exceptionally low levels for the federal funds rate at least until late [year+2]. The Committee will closely monitor new data on economic and financial developments and will implement further accommodation as necessary to foster a stronger economic recovery and sustained improvements in the labor market within a stable price environment.
