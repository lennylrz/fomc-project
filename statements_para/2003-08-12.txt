The Committee chose to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

The Committee assesses that the current supportive monetary policy, alongside strong underlying productivity gains, continues to bolster economic activity. Recent data indicates an increase in spending, though employment metrics present a mixed picture. Businesses' ability to raise prices and core consumer price increases remain subdued.

Regarding the economic outlook for the coming quarters, the Committee views the potential for stronger or weaker growth as roughly balanced. However, the likelihood of an undesirable decline in inflation, while small, is greater than the chance of inflation rising from its present low level. The Committee concludes that, for the foreseeable future, the primary concern will likely be the risk of inflation falling too low. Given these conditions, the Committee believes that the current accommodative policy stance can be sustained for an extended duration.
