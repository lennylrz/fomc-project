The Committee chose to maintain its policy rate at its current level.

Economic expansion has decelerated throughout the year, partly due to a slowdown in the housing sector. Looking ahead, the economy is expected to grow at a modest rate.

Underlying inflation measures have been high, and the economy's full capacity utilization could sustain inflationary pressures. However, these pressures are anticipated to ease over time, driven by less impact from energy costs, stable inflation expectations, and the combined effects of monetary policy actions and other factors limiting overall demand.

Nevertheless, the Committee believes that some inflation risks persist. The necessity, degree, and timing of any further tightening to address these risks will depend on how the inflation and economic growth outlook evolves, based on new information.
