Today, the Committee decided to increase its benchmark interest rate by half a percentage point. Concurrently, the Board of Governors endorsed a similar half-percentage-point rise in the rate at which financial institutions can borrow from the central bank.

The Committee observes that the expansion of overall spending continues to outstrip the swift improvements in the economy's capacity to produce goods and services, which are driven by efficiency gains. This ongoing imbalance between robust demand and limited supply is a source of concern, as it could lead to inflationary pressures that would jeopardize the economy's current strong performance.

Considering its objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current information, the Committee assesses that the likelihood of future inflationary pressures is elevated.
