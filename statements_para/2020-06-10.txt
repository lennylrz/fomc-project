The Committee chose to keep its policy interest rate unchanged.

The [event] pandemic has inflicted severe human and economic distress globally and within the United States. The virus and public health interventions have led to steep drops in economic activity and a sharp rise in unemployment. Reduced demand and significantly lower oil costs are suppressing consumer price increases. Financial conditions have improved, partly due to policy actions aimed at bolstering the economy and credit availability for American households and businesses.

The current public health emergency will significantly impact economic activity, employment, and inflation in the short term, and presents substantial dangers to the economic outlook over the medium term.

The Committee anticipates maintaining this target range until it is certain that the economy has overcome recent challenges and is progressing toward its objectives of maximum employment and price stability. The Committee will continue to observe the implications of new data for the economic outlook, including public health information, global trends, and subdued inflationary pressures. It will employ its instruments and take suitable action to support the economy. When deciding on the timing and magnitude of future adjustments to monetary policy, the Committee will evaluate actual and anticipated economic conditions against its maximum employment goal and its symmetrical 2 percent inflation target. This evaluation will incorporate a broad array of data, such as labor market indicators, measures of inflationary pressures and expectations, and financial and international developments.

To facilitate credit flow to households and businesses, the Federal Reserve will, in the coming months, expand its holdings of Treasury securities and agency residential and commercial mortgage-backed securities at least at the current rate to ensure smooth market operations, thereby promoting the effective transmission of monetary policy to broader financial conditions. Furthermore, the Open Market Desk will persist in offering extensive overnight and term repurchase agreement operations. The Committee will closely track developments and stands ready to modify its plans as necessary.
