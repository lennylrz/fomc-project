The Committee has opted to decrease its primary policy interest rate by half a percentage point. Concurrently, the Board of Governors sanctioned a reduction in the discount rate by a quarter of a percentage point, aligning with proposals from several regional Federal Reserve Banks. The Board also signaled its readiness to endorse an additional quarter-percentage-point decrease in the discount rate should further requests be submitted by Federal Reserve Banks.

This adjustment reflects a continued downturn in commercial activity and manufacturing output. Contributing factors include diminished consumer sentiment, constrained conditions within certain financial sectors, and elevated energy costs that are eroding the spending capacity of households and businesses. Despite these challenges, inflationary pressures remain subdued. Furthermore, there is currently little indication that the long-term progress in technological innovation and the resulting productivity enhancements are diminishing.

Considering its objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current information, the Committee maintains that the predominant risks lean towards an environment of economic deceleration in the near term.
