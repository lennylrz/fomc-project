The Committee chose to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

Financial sector pressures have intensified considerably, and the employment situation has deteriorated further. Recent economic expansion seems to have decelerated, partly due to a reduction in consumer outlays. Restrictive credit conditions, the continued downturn in the housing sector, and a slight slowdown in international sales are anticipated to hinder economic expansion in the coming quarters. Eventually, the considerable loosening of monetary policy, alongside continuous efforts to enhance market liquidity, should aid in fostering moderate economic expansion.

Inflation has been elevated, driven by previous surges in energy and certain other commodity prices. The Committee foresees inflation easing later this year and into next year, though the inflation outlook remains highly unpredictable.

The Committee is significantly concerned by both the risks of slower growth and the risks of higher inflation. The Committee will closely observe economic and financial trends and will intervene as necessary to support sustained economic growth and stable prices.
