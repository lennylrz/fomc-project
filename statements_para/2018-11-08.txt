The Committee has opted to keep its primary policy interest rate unchanged.

Recent data indicates continued strengthening in the employment situation, with robust job creation over the past few months and a lower unemployment rate. Economic activity has been expanding at a vigorous pace. Consumer spending has maintained its strong upward trend, though the expansion of business capital expenditures has slowed from its earlier rapid rate this year. Over the past year, both overall price increases and those excluding volatile food and energy components have remained close to the 2 percent target. Measures of long-term inflation expectations have shown little overall change.

The Committee anticipates that additional, measured increases in the policy rate will align with ongoing economic expansion, a healthy job market, and inflation stabilizing around the Committee's 2 percent symmetric target over the medium term. The potential threats to the economic outlook appear generally even.

Future adjustments to the policy rate's level and timing will be determined by evaluating current and projected economic conditions against the Committee's goals of maximum employment and 2 percent symmetric inflation. This evaluation will incorporate diverse information, including labor market metrics, indicators of price pressures and inflation expectations, and developments in financial markets and global economies.
