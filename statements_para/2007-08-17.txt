The Federal Open Market Committee decided to maintain its current stance on the federal funds rate target.

This decision comes as financial market conditions have worsened, and the tightening of credit, alongside heightened uncertainty, could impede economic expansion in the future. While recent economic indicators point to continued moderate growth, the Committee believes that the risks to this growth are now significantly skewed to the downside.

The Committee is closely observing these developments and stands ready to intervene if necessary to lessen any negative impacts on the economy stemming from the turmoil in financial markets.
