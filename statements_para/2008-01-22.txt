The Committee has opted to reduce its primary policy interest rate by three-quarters of a percentage point.

This adjustment reflects a less favorable economic outlook and growing threats to expansion. Although some pressures in short-term funding markets have lessened, overall financial conditions have worsened, and access to credit has become more difficult for certain businesses and individuals. Furthermore, recent data points to a deepening downturn in the housing sector and some softening in employment.

Inflation is anticipated to ease in the upcoming quarters, but close observation of price trends will remain essential.

Significant downside risks to economic growth persist. The Committee will continue to evaluate how financial and other factors influence future economic conditions and will respond promptly as required to mitigate these risks.

Separately, the Board of Governors endorsed a three-quarters of a percentage point reduction in the discount rate. This action followed requests from the Federal Reserve Banks of Chicago and Minneapolis.
