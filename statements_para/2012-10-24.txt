The Committee decided to maintain its current policy rate.

Recent data indicates that economic activity has expanded moderately in recent months. Job growth has been sluggish, and the unemployment rate remains elevated. Consumer spending has picked up somewhat, but business fixed investment has slowed. The housing market shows further, albeit modest, improvement. Inflation has recently risen, driven by higher energy prices. Long-term inflation expectations remain stable.

The Committee is concerned that, without sufficient support, economic growth may not be strong enough to achieve sustained improvement in the labor market. Global financial market strains continue to pose significant downside risks to the economic outlook. Inflation is expected to be at or below the 2 percent objective over the medium term.

To support a stronger recovery and ensure inflation aligns with its 2 percent objective, the Committee will continue purchasing agency mortgage-backed securities at a pace of $40 billion per month. It will also continue its program to extend the average maturity of its Treasury holdings through the end of the year, and maintain its policy of reinvesting principal payments from agency debt and mortgage-backed securities into agency mortgage-backed securities. These actions, which will increase longer-term security holdings by about $85 billion each month through the end of the year, aim to lower long-term interest rates, support mortgage markets, and ease broader financial conditions.

The Committee will closely monitor incoming economic and financial information. If the labor market outlook does not substantially improve, the Committee will continue its purchases of agency mortgage-backed securities, undertake additional asset purchases, and use other policy tools as appropriate, within a context of price stability. The Committee will consider the efficacy and costs of such purchases when determining their size, pace, and composition.

A highly accommodative monetary policy stance will likely be appropriate for a considerable time after the economic recovery strengthens. Exceptionally low levels for the policy rate are anticipated to be warranted at least through mid-[year+3].
