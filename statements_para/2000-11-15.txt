The Committee chose to maintain its current monetary policy settings, leaving the federal funds rate target unchanged.

The labor market continues to exhibit exceptional tightness. While the recent surge in energy costs has not yet significantly impacted underlying price measures, it poses a risk of elevating inflation expectations. Consequently, the Committee still perceives a potential for increased inflationary pressures. However, a recent deceleration in consumer and business spending, alongside a tightening of financial conditions, indicates that economic expansion might proceed at a rate below its long-term sustainable potential for a period.

Despite these developments, the observed moderation in demand has not been substantial enough to alter the Committee's assessment. Given its long-term objectives of stable prices and sustained economic growth, and based on current information, the Committee continues to believe that the balance of risks leans primarily towards conditions that could lead to higher inflation in the near future.
