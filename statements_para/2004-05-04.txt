The Committee has opted to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

Economic activity continues to expand at a healthy pace, supported by an accommodating monetary policy and strong productivity gains. Recent information suggests sustained output growth and an increase in employment. While recent price data show some upward movement, long-term inflation expectations remain stable.

Looking ahead, the Committee assesses that the potential for stronger or weaker economic growth over the coming quarters is evenly balanced. Likewise, the risks to achieving stable prices are now considered to be in equilibrium. Given the current low inflation and underutilized resources, the Committee anticipates that adjustments to monetary policy will likely occur gradually.
