The Committee has opted to decrease its primary interest rate by half a percentage point. Concurrently, the Board of Governors has sanctioned a similar reduction in the discount rate.

The Committee maintains its view that a supportive monetary policy, alongside strong underlying productivity growth, continues to bolster economic activity. Nevertheless, recent economic indicators suggest that increased uncertainty, partly due to elevated geopolitical tensions, is currently dampening consumer and business spending, output, and job creation. Price increases and anticipated future price increases remain under control.

Given these conditions, the Committee anticipates that this additional monetary stimulus will assist the economy in navigating its current period of weakness. With this adjustment, the Committee assesses that, considering its long-term objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current information, the risks to achieving both goals in the near future are evenly distributed.

Regarding the discount rate adjustment, the Federal Reserve Board endorsed the proposals from the Federal Reserve Banks of Dallas and New York.
