The Committee has opted to retain its current monetary policy settings, leaving the federal funds rate target unaltered.

Economic expansion appears to be decelerating further, influenced by several factors. Elevated energy expenses are dampening both consumer demand and corporate profitability. There are widespread accounts of significant underperformance in sales and earnings, alongside instability in certain parts of the financial system. Although some inflationary pressures remain, they are less pronounced due to the slower economic pace and the absence of any rise in long-term inflation expectations. The Committee will continue to closely observe economic developments.

Considering its objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current data, the Committee assesses that the primary risks lean towards a period of economic softness in the near future.
