Today, the Committee chose to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

The Committee assesses that the economy continues to benefit from supportive monetary conditions and strong underlying productivity gains. Recent data confirm that economic output is expanding at a healthy rate. While the pace of job losses has slowed, the rate of new employment has not kept up. Core consumer price increases remain subdued and are anticipated to stay low.

Looking ahead, the Committee views the potential for stronger or weaker sustainable growth over the coming quarters as roughly balanced. The likelihood of an undesirable decline in inflation has decreased recently and is now seen as nearly equivalent to the chance of an inflation increase. Given the low inflation environment and underutilized resources, the Committee believes it can proceed cautiously in adjusting its accommodative stance.
