The Committee decided to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

To ensure credit availability for households and businesses, the Federal Reserve will continue its efforts to stabilize the Treasury and agency mortgage-backed securities markets. This involves ongoing purchases of these securities in quantities sufficient to facilitate orderly market operations and effective transmission of monetary policy to broader financial conditions. The Committee will also incorporate agency commercial mortgage-backed securities into its agency mortgage-backed security purchases. Furthermore, large-scale overnight and term repurchase agreement operations will persist. The Committee will closely monitor market conditions and determine the appropriate pace of its securities acquisitions at upcoming meetings.

All members present, including the Chair and Vice Chair, voted in favor of this monetary policy action.
