The Committee decided to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

Economic activity and employment indicators have shown continued improvement, supported by vaccination efforts and robust policy measures. While sectors most impacted by the pandemic have improved, they have not fully recovered. Inflation has increased, primarily due to temporary factors. Financial conditions generally remain supportive, partly reflecting policies designed to aid the economy and credit availability for households and businesses.

The economic trajectory remains dependent on the virus's progression. Ongoing vaccination progress will likely lessen the public health crisis's economic impact, though risks to the outlook persist.

The Committee aims for maximum employment and a 2 percent inflation rate over the long term. Given sustained inflation below this long-run target, the Committee intends to achieve inflation moderately above 2 percent for a period, ensuring inflation averages 2 percent over time and long-term inflation expectations remain firmly anchored at 2 percent. An accommodative monetary policy stance will be maintained until these outcomes are realized. The Committee anticipates keeping its policy rate unchanged until labor market conditions align with its maximum employment assessment and inflation reaches 2 percent, on track to moderately surpass 2 percent for some time. Since [month], the Committee has continued to expand its holdings of Treasury securities and agency mortgage-backed securities at a monthly pace of at least $80 billion and $40 billion, respectively, until substantial further progress toward its employment and price stability objectives is achieved. Progress toward these goals has occurred, and the Committee will evaluate further progress in upcoming meetings. These asset acquisitions contribute to smooth market operations and supportive financial conditions, facilitating credit flow to households and businesses.

The Committee will continue to monitor incoming data's implications for the economic outlook when determining the appropriate monetary policy stance. Should risks arise that could hinder the achievement of its objectives, the Committee stands ready to adjust policy as needed. Its evaluations will consider various information, including public health data, labor market conditions, inflationary pressures and expectations, and financial and international developments.
