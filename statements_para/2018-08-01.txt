The Committee chose to keep its primary interest rate unchanged at this gathering. This decision reflects current and anticipated conditions in the job market and price levels. The current monetary policy stance continues to support robust employment and a sustained return to the 2 percent inflation target.

Recent data since the [month] meeting show continued strengthening in the job market and a vigorous pace of economic expansion. Employment gains have been substantial over recent months, and the jobless rate remains low. Consumer spending and business capital expenditures have shown strong growth. Over the past year, both overall inflation and core inflation, which excludes food and energy, are close to 2 percent. Measures of long-term inflation expectations have, on the whole, seen little alteration.

The Committee's mandate is to promote maximum employment and stable prices. The Committee anticipates that additional, measured increases in the policy rate will align with ongoing economic growth, healthy labor market conditions, and inflation stabilizing around its symmetric 2 percent goal over the medium term. The potential risks to the economic outlook appear generally even.

When considering future adjustments to the policy rate, the Committee will evaluate economic conditions against its goals for maximum employment and symmetric 2 percent inflation. This evaluation will incorporate various data, including labor market indicators, inflation pressures and expectations, and financial and global developments.
