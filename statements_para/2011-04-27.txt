The Committee decided to keep its policy interest rate unchanged.

Recent data indicate that the economy is recovering at a moderate speed, and the job market is slowly getting better. Consumer spending and business investment in equipment and software are growing. However, investment in commercial buildings remains weak, and the housing market is still struggling. Commodity prices have increased substantially since last summer, and worries about global oil supplies have led to further oil price hikes since the last meeting. Inflation has risen recently, but long-term inflation expectations are stable, and core inflation measures are still low.

The Committee aims to achieve maximum employment and stable prices. The unemployment rate is high, and core inflation measures are somewhat low compared to what the Committee considers consistent with its long-term goals. Recent increases in energy and commodity prices have boosted inflation. The Committee expects these effects to be temporary but will closely watch inflation and inflation expectations. The Committee still anticipates a gradual return to higher resource utilization while maintaining price stability.

To support a stronger recovery and ensure inflation aligns with its mandate over time, the Committee will continue to expand its securities holdings as announced in [month]. Specifically, the Committee will continue reinvesting principal payments from its securities and will finish purchasing $600 billion of longer-term Treasury securities by the end of the current quarter. The Committee will regularly assess its securities holdings based on new information and is ready to adjust them as needed to best promote maximum employment and price stability.

The Committee expects that economic conditions, including low resource utilization, subdued inflation, and stable inflation expectations, will likely necessitate exceptionally low policy rates for an extended period.

The Committee will continue to observe the economic outlook and financial developments and will use its policy tools as needed to support the economic recovery and ensure inflation aligns with its mandate over time.
