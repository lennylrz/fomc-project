The Committee has opted to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent data indicate a slight uptick in economic expansion, with early indications of stability emerging within the housing sector. The broader economy is anticipated to grow at a measured speed in the upcoming periods.

Core inflation figures have shown modest improvement recently, and inflationary pressures are expected to ease over time. Nevertheless, the economy's high utilization of its productive capacity could potentially keep inflation elevated.

The Committee perceives that certain inflation-related risks persist. The necessity, magnitude, and timing of any further policy tightening to address these risks will be determined by how the future prospects for both inflation and economic growth evolve, as suggested by new information.
