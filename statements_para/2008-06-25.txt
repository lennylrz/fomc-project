The Committee chose to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent data suggest continued expansion in the broader economy, partly due to an uptick in consumer outlays. Nevertheless, employment conditions have further deteriorated, and financial markets remain under significant strain. Restrictive lending conditions, the ongoing downturn in the housing sector, and rising energy costs are anticipated to constrain economic expansion in the coming quarters.

The Committee projects a deceleration in the rate of price increases later this year and into [year+1]. However, given persistent increases in energy and certain other commodity prices, alongside elevated readings of some inflation expectation metrics, the future path of inflation remains highly uncertain.

The significant monetary policy accommodation already implemented, coupled with ongoing efforts to enhance market liquidity, should contribute to achieving moderate growth over time. While risks to economic contraction persist, they appear somewhat reduced, whereas the potential for higher inflation and inflation expectations has grown. The Committee will continue to observe economic and financial trends and will intervene as necessary to foster sustained economic growth and stable prices.

One member, [member], dissented, favoring an upward adjustment to the policy interest rate at this meeting.
