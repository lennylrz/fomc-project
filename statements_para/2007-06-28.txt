The Committee has opted to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

Economic expansion during the initial six months of the year was modest, even with the ongoing changes in the housing market. Projections suggest this moderate growth will persist in the near future.

Recent data indicate a slight improvement in underlying inflation. However, clear evidence of a sustained reduction in inflationary pressures is still lacking. Furthermore, the economy's high utilization of resources could perpetuate these pressures.

Given these conditions, the Committee's primary concern remains the possibility that inflation may not decrease as anticipated. Future adjustments to policy will be determined by how new information shapes the outlook for both inflation and economic growth.
