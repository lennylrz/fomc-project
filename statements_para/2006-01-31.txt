The Committee decided to increase its primary policy interest rate by a quarter of a percentage point.

Recent economic indicators have shown variability, yet overall economic expansion seems robust. Underlying inflation has remained relatively subdued in recent months, and long-term inflation outlooks are stable. However, potential increases in the utilization of productive capacity and elevated energy costs could contribute to inflationary pressures.

The Committee believes that additional policy tightening might be necessary to maintain an appropriate balance between the risks to sustained economic growth and stable prices. Regardless, the Committee will adjust its approach based on evolving economic conditions to achieve its goals.

Separately, the Board of Governors unanimously endorsed a 25-basis-point increase in the discount rate. This action followed requests from the boards of directors of numerous Federal Reserve Banks.
