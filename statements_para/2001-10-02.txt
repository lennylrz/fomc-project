The Committee chose to decrease its benchmark interest rate by half a percentage point today. Concurrently, the Board of Governors sanctioned a similar half-percentage-point reduction in the discount rate.

The recent [event] has introduced considerable unpredictability into an already fragile economic landscape. This situation is further suppressing consumer and business expenditures. Despite these immediate challenges, the long-term outlook for economic expansion and productivity gains remains positive and is expected to materialize once the current constraints on demand diminish.

The Committee maintains its view that, considering its objectives for stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current data, the predominant risks lean towards conditions that could lead to economic softness in the near term.

Regarding the discount rate adjustment, the Federal Reserve Board endorsed proposals from the Boards of Directors of the Federal Reserve Banks of Boston, New York, Cleveland, Richmond, Atlanta, St. Louis, Kansas City, and San Francisco.
