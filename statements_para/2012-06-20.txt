The Committee has chosen to maintain its primary policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent data indicates that the economy has been expanding at a moderate pace this year. However, job creation has slowed recently, and the unemployment rate remains elevated. Business investment in fixed assets has continued to grow. Household spending appears to be increasing somewhat more slowly than earlier in the year. Despite some positive signs, the housing sector remains weak. Inflation has decreased, primarily due to lower crude oil and gasoline prices, and long-term inflation expectations have remained stable.

The Committee expects economic growth to remain moderate in the near future, gradually accelerating thereafter. As a result, the Committee anticipates a slow decline in the unemployment rate towards levels consistent with its objectives. Furthermore, global financial market stresses continue to present significant downside risks to the economic outlook. The Committee expects inflation over the medium term to be at or below its target rate.

To support a more robust economic recovery and ensure inflation aligns with its long-term objective, the Committee intends to maintain a very accommodative monetary policy stance. The Committee currently anticipates that economic conditions, including underutilized resources and a subdued medium-term inflation outlook, will likely necessitate exceptionally low interest rates at least through late [year+2].

The Committee also decided to extend its program to lengthen the average maturity of its securities holdings until the end of the year. This involves purchasing longer-dated Treasury securities at the current rate and selling or redeeming an equivalent amount of shorter-dated Treasury securities. This program aims to reduce longer-term interest rates and foster more accommodative financial conditions. The Committee will continue its existing practice of reinvesting principal payments from agency debt and mortgage-backed securities into agency mortgage-backed securities. The Committee stands ready to take additional steps as needed to promote a stronger economic recovery and sustained improvement in labor market conditions while maintaining price stability.

One member, [member], dissented from this action, opposing the continuation of the maturity extension program.
