The Committee has opted to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

Economic expansion experienced a deceleration earlier this year, and the housing market continues its rebalancing. Despite this, the economy is anticipated to grow at a measured speed in the near future.

Underlying inflation persists at a somewhat elevated level. While inflationary pressures are expected to ease eventually, the current high utilization of economic resources could prolong these pressures.

Given these conditions, the Committee's primary concern remains the possibility that inflation may not decrease as projected. Future adjustments to policy will be determined by how the outlook for both inflation and economic growth develops, based on new information received.
