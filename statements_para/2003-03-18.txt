The Committee chose to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent information on the economy has presented a varied picture since the [month] gathering, with particular weakness observed in employment figures. Nevertheless, the Committee attributes the current sluggishness in economic expansion largely to elevated oil prices and other geopolitical unknowns. As these uncertainties dissipate, a development widely anticipated, the Committee expects that the current monetary policy stance, alongside continued productivity gains, will adequately bolster economic activity, leading to a gradual improvement in the economic environment.

Given the significant short-term geopolitical uncertainties and their apparent impact on economic choices, the Committee finds itself unable to meaningfully assess the current balance of risks concerning its long-term objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion. Consequently, the Committee has opted to defer such an assessment until some of these uncertainties diminish. Close monitoring of developments is especially valuable in the present climate.
