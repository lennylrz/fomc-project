The Committee chose to maintain its benchmark interest rate at its current level.

Economic expansion proceeded at a measured pace over the initial six months of the year. Recent weeks have seen instability in financial markets, a tightening of credit availability for certain consumers and businesses, and the continued adjustment in the housing sector. Despite these factors, the economy is projected to sustain a moderate growth trajectory in the upcoming quarters, bolstered by strong job creation, rising incomes, and a vigorous global economic environment.

Underlying inflation metrics have shown slight improvement recently. However, clear evidence of a lasting reduction in inflationary pressures has not yet emerged. Furthermore, the economy's high capacity utilization could perpetuate these pressures.

While the potential for slower growth has somewhat increased, the Committee's primary concern remains the possibility that inflation will not decrease as anticipated. Future adjustments to monetary policy will be determined by incoming data regarding both inflation and economic expansion.
