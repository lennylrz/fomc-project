Underlying inflation metrics have been high, and the current tight capacity utilization along with elevated energy and commodity prices could keep inflationary pressures persistent. However, these pressures are expected to ease over time, as energy prices provide less upward push, inflation expectations remain anchored, and the combined impact of past monetary policy actions and other factors dampens overall demand.

Despite this, the Committee believes that some inflation-related dangers persist. The necessity, extent, and timing of any further tightening to mitigate these dangers will be determined by how the inflation and economic growth forecasts evolve, based on new data.
