The Committee decided to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent data indicate that economic expansion is proceeding at a moderate rate, though this is somewhat slower than previously projected. Labor market indicators have also been weaker than anticipated. This slower growth is partly due to temporary factors, such as the impact of increased food and energy costs on consumer spending power and disruptions in supply chains stemming from recent events in [country]. Consumer spending and business investment in equipment and software are still growing. However, investment in commercial structures remains weak, and the housing sector continues to be subdued. Inflation has risen recently, primarily reflecting higher prices for certain commodities and imported goods, alongside the aforementioned supply chain issues. Nevertheless, long-term inflation expectations have held steady.

The Committee aims to achieve maximum employment and price stability, in line with its legal obligations. The unemployment rate is still high; however, the Committee expects the recovery to accelerate in the coming quarters, leading to a gradual reduction in the unemployment rate towards levels consistent with its dual mandate. While inflation has recently increased, the Committee anticipates it will decline to levels at or below those consistent with its dual mandate as the effects of past energy and commodity price increases fade. The Committee will, however, closely monitor inflation and inflation expectations.

To support the ongoing economic recovery and ensure inflation aligns with its mandate over time, the Committee will conclude its purchases of longer-term Treasury securities by the end of this month and will continue its practice of reinvesting principal payments from its securities. The Committee expects that economic conditions, including underutilized resources and a moderate inflation outlook in the medium term, will likely necessitate exceptionally low policy rates for an extended duration. The Committee will regularly assess the size and composition of its securities holdings and is prepared to modify them as needed.

The Committee will observe the economic outlook and financial developments, taking necessary actions to best promote maximum employment and price stability.
