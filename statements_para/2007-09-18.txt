The Committee has opted to decrease its primary policy interest rate by half a percentage point.

Economic expansion was subdued in the initial six months of the year. The current tightening of lending standards could exacerbate the downturn in the housing sector and generally impede broader economic activity. This adjustment aims to mitigate some negative impacts on the wider economy that might stem from financial market turbulence, thereby supporting sustained, moderate growth.

Core inflation metrics have shown slight improvement this year. Nevertheless, the Committee believes that certain inflation-related dangers persist and will diligently track price developments.

Since the previous scheduled gathering, financial market events have heightened the ambiguity surrounding future economic conditions. The Committee will persist in evaluating how these and other factors influence economic projections and will intervene as necessary to maintain price stability and foster enduring economic expansion.

Concurrently, the Board of Governors unanimously endorsed a half-percentage-point reduction in the discount rate. This decision followed the approval of requests from the Boards of Directors of several Federal Reserve Banks.
