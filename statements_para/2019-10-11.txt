The Committee decided to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

To ensure sufficient reserves within the financial system, especially considering upcoming increases in the Federal Reserve's non-reserve obligations, the Federal Reserve will acquire Treasury bills. These acquisitions will continue at least through the second quarter of next year, aiming to keep reserve balances at or above the level observed in early [month] [this year].

Furthermore, the Federal Reserve will implement both term and overnight repurchase agreements. These operations will extend at least through [month] of next year. Their purpose is to guarantee ample reserves, even when non-reserve liabilities rise sharply, and to alleviate potential money market stresses that could hinder effective policy execution.

These measures are technical adjustments designed to support the smooth functioning of monetary policy and do not signify a shift in the overall policy stance. The Committee will monitor money market conditions closely to determine the optimal level of reserves for efficient policy implementation and is prepared to modify these plans as needed.

In support of these actions, the Federal Open Market Committee unanimously authorized the Federal Reserve Bank of New York to conduct System Open Market Account transactions. This includes maintaining the federal funds rate within its established target range. The Desk is also directed to purchase Treasury bills as specified above and to conduct term and overnight repurchase operations as outlined. Additionally, the Desk will conduct overnight reverse repurchase operations at an offering rate of 1.70 percent, limited by available Treasury securities and a per-counterparty cap of $30 billion daily.

The Desk will continue to reinvest all principal payments from maturing Treasury securities at auction. Principal payments from agency debt and agency mortgage-backed securities will also continue to be reinvested monthly. Up to $20 billion of these payments will be reinvested in Treasury securities to align with the maturity profile of outstanding Treasuries; amounts exceeding $20 billion will be reinvested in agency mortgage-backed securities. Minor operational deviations are permissible. The Desk is also authorized to engage in dollar roll and coupon swap transactions to facilitate agency mortgage-backed securities settlements.
