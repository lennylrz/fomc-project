The Committee chose to keep its policy interest rate unchanged.

The current health crisis has inflicted immense human and economic suffering both domestically and globally. The spread of the virus and necessary public health interventions are leading to steep drops in economic activity and a rapid increase in unemployment. Reduced demand and substantially lower oil prices are suppressing consumer price increases. Disruptions to economic activity, both domestically and internationally, have significantly impacted financial markets and hindered credit availability for American households and businesses.

The persistent public health emergency will severely constrain economic activity, employment levels, and price stability in the immediate future, and presents substantial dangers to the economic forecast over the medium term.

The Committee anticipates maintaining this policy rate until it is assured that the economy has overcome recent challenges and is progressing toward its objectives of maximum employment and price stability. The Committee will continue to evaluate incoming data for its implications on the economic outlook, including public health information, global trends, and subdued inflationary pressures. It will employ its instruments and take suitable actions to bolster the economy. When deciding on the timing and magnitude of future adjustments to monetary policy, the Committee will evaluate actual and projected economic conditions against its maximum employment goal and its symmetrical 2 percent inflation target. This evaluation will incorporate a broad spectrum of data, such as labor market indicators, measures of inflation pressures and expectations, and financial and international developments.

To facilitate credit flow to households and businesses, the Federal Reserve will persist in acquiring Treasury securities and agency residential and commercial mortgage-backed securities in quantities necessary to ensure orderly market operations, thereby promoting the effective transmission of monetary policy to broader financial conditions. Furthermore, the Open Market Desk will continue to conduct extensive overnight and term repurchase agreement operations. The Committee will closely observe market conditions and stands ready to modify its strategies as appropriate.
