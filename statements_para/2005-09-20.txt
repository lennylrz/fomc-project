While elevated energy and other expenses could fuel inflationary pressures, core inflation has been relatively subdued recently, and long-term inflation expectations remain stable.

The Committee judges that, with suitable monetary policy adjustments, the potential for both stronger and weaker outcomes for sustained growth and stable prices should be balanced. Given that underlying inflation is anticipated to remain in check, the Committee believes that the process of reducing monetary accommodation can proceed at a steady pace. Nevertheless, the Committee will adjust its approach as economic conditions evolve to meet its commitment to maintaining price stability.
