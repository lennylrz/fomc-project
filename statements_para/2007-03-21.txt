The Committee has opted to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

Economic data received recently presents a varied picture, and the housing market continues its rebalancing. Despite this, the economy is projected to sustain a moderate expansion in the near future.

Underlying inflation metrics have shown some elevation recently. While inflationary pressures are anticipated to ease eventually, the current high utilization of economic capacity could prolong these pressures.

Given these conditions, the Committee's primary concern remains the possibility that inflation may not decline as anticipated. Future adjustments to policy will be determined by how new information shapes the outlook for both price stability and economic expansion.
