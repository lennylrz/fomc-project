The Committee has opted to decrease its primary policy interest rate by a quarter of a percentage point. Concurrently, the Board of Governors sanctioned a similar reduction in the discount rate.

Consumer spending has remained robust. However, corporate earnings and investment in new equipment continue to decline, and international economic expansion is decelerating, which is impacting the domestic economy. This situation is anticipated to alleviate inflationary pressures in both employment and goods markets.

Despite positive long-term outlooks for economic efficiency and overall growth, the Committee still perceives that, given its objectives for stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current information, the predominant risks lean towards potential economic softening in the near future.
