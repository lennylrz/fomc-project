The Committee has opted to retain its current monetary policy settings, leaving the federal funds rate target unaltered.

Economic indicators suggest that the expansion of overall demand might be slowing to a rate more aligned with the economy's productive capacity. While underlying price measures show a modest acceleration compared to last year, significant gains in productivity are helping to curb expenses and mitigate fundamental inflationary forces.

However, evidence indicating a sustainable trajectory for demand growth remains uncertain and initial. Furthermore, the employment of available labor resources continues at an exceptionally elevated level.

Given these conditions, and considering its objectives of stable prices and sustained economic expansion, along with current information, the Committee judges that the risks predominantly lean towards circumstances that could lead to increased inflationary pressures in the near future.
