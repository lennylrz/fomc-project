The Committee chose to maintain its policy interest rate at its current level.

Recent data indicates ongoing expansion in economic activity. Nevertheless, the boost from inventory adjustments and the pace of final demand growth seem to have slowed. The Committee anticipates an acceleration in final demand over the next few quarters, partly due to strong productivity gains, though the extent of this strengthening is unclear.

Given these conditions, and despite the current supportive monetary policy, the Committee judges that, looking ahead, the risks to achieving both price stability and sustainable economic growth are evenly weighted, based on available information.

No members dissented from this decision.
