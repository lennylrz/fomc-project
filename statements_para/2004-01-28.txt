The Committee chose to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

The Committee continues to assess that its current monetary policy approach, alongside strong underlying productivity gains, is significantly bolstering economic activity. Information gathered since the last meeting confirms a rapid expansion in output. While new job creation remains modest, other metrics indicate an improving employment situation. Core consumer price increases are subdued and are projected to stay low.

The Committee judges that the potential for stronger or weaker sustainable growth over the coming quarters is roughly balanced. The likelihood of an undesirable decline in inflation has lessened recently and now appears nearly equivalent to the chance of an inflation increase. Given the very low inflation and underutilized resources, the Committee believes it can proceed cautiously in adjusting its policy stance.
