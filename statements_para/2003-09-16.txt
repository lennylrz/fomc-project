Today, the Committee chose to maintain its primary interest rate at its current level.

The Committee assesses that the present monetary policy approach, which is supportive, combined with strong underlying productivity gains, continues to significantly bolster economic activity. Data gathered since the last meeting indicates a strengthening in consumer and business outlays, despite a softening in the employment landscape. Firms' ability to set prices and the core measure of consumer price increases remain subdued.

Regarding the economic trajectory for the upcoming quarters, the Committee views the potential for stronger or weaker growth as roughly balanced. However, the likelihood of an undesirable decrease in inflation, while small, is greater than the chance of inflation rising from its current low point. Overall, the Committee concludes that the risk of inflation falling too low remains the primary concern for the foreseeable future. Given these conditions, the Committee believes that the current accommodative policy stance can persist for an extended duration.
