The Committee chose to decrease its primary interest rate by a quarter of a percentage point. Concurrently, the Board of Governors sanctioned a similar reduction in the discount rate.

Economic activity continues to be constrained by several factors observed over recent periods: a downturn in corporate earnings and investment in capital by businesses, sluggish expansion in household spending, and decelerating international growth. This environment is anticipated to maintain price stability due to reduced pressures in employment and goods markets.

Despite ongoing positive developments that support future productivity gains and overall economic health, the Committee maintains its view that, considering its objectives for stable prices and sustained economic expansion, and based on current data, the predominant risks lean towards potential economic softness in the near term.
